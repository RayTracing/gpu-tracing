Contributing
====================================================================================================
All corrections and improvements are very much appreciated. Please feel free to open a PR.

I don't expect major contributions at the moment, at least until the first draft is mostly
complete. If you have suggestions or ideas for a new chapter or a revision, please don't hesitate
to start a [discussion](https://github.com/RayTracing/gpu-tracing/discussions) or open an issue or
a draft PR.
